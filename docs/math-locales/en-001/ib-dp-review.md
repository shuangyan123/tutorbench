# International Baccalaureate DP mathematics — global English review

**Scope:** IB Mathematics: Analysis and Approaches (AA) and Applications and
Interpretation (AI), both at **Standard Level (SL)** and **Higher Level (HL)**.
See [official IB mathematics](https://ibo.org/programmes/diploma-programme/curriculum/mathematics/).

**Cohort boundary:** IB identifies the current DP mathematics course assessment
through **November 2028** and a revised mathematics curriculum first assessed
in **2029**. Do not treat the future guide as an instruction to revise a 2026 case.

## Distinct exam/course pathways

| Pathway | Review emphasis | Possible synthetic Tutor check | Gaps |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| AA SL | Algebraic, functional, geometric and calculus reasoning; appropriate justification | `mc-composition-domain` | IB-authentic contextualisation and exploration |
| AA HL | Further analytical depth; multi-step, non-routine argument | `mc-induction-recurrence` as a proof-prompt trial only | Detailed HL-specific content alignment |
| AI SL | Modelling, interpretation, statistics and technology-appropriate methods | `mc-percent-reversal`, `mc-simpson-aggregation` | Authentic data-modelling and tool tasks |
| AI HL | Additional applied reasoning and mathematical content | `mc-simpson-aggregation` as interpretation probe | HL-specific advanced modelling/analysis |

These are **authoring emphases**, not claims that these synthetic cases are
IB papers or that particular synthetic questions are required in each route.
AA and AI are distinct mathematical intentions, not universally "hard" and "easy".

## Why global English rather than one country's curriculum?

IB operates internationally, so the English review uses IB's own
course names, SL/HL and official subject briefs. A single language is
appropriate for this reviewer edition, but local schooling contexts can
still differ: mathematical notations, previous curricula, and available
teaching resources should be treated as student/context evidence.

Tutor evaluation: a correct calculation without valid model assumptions,
interpretation, or reflection can be inadequate in an application task.
On an independently assessed student response, correctness and expected
working must be separate from the Tutor's decision to hint, probe, or model.

**Evidence requirement:** any future IB-specific score needs item/marking
descriptors for the precise course and assessment year plus genuine independent
work. Current cases measure only authored conversational behaviour.
