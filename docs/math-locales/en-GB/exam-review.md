# GCSE and Cambridge International mathematics — reviewer guide (en-GB)

**Audience:** UK and Cambridge International exam reviewers. English terminology
does not imply that AQA GCSE, Cambridge IGCSE and Cambridge AS/A Level share
the same syllabus.

## AQA GCSE Mathematics (8300)

Use the [official AQA 8300 specification](https://www.aqa.org.uk/subjects/mathematics/gcse/mathematics-8300/specification/specification-at-a-glance)
and check the relevant **Foundation** or **Higher** tier. Content commonly
includes number, algebra, ratio/proportion/rates, geometry/measures,
probability and statistics. Retain different calculator/non-calculator
papers and written-working expectations rather than forcing one "difficulty"
label across tiers.

- **Synthetic example:** `mc-similarity-area` tests whether a Tutor explains
  the square of a linear scale factor using known area reasoning. It is not
  itself a question from AQA 8300.
- **Gap:** constructions, statistical interpretation, geometry diagrams,
  data representation and suitable tier-specific mathematical communication.

## Cambridge IGCSE Mathematics (0580)

Use the [Cambridge 0580 syllabus by examination year](https://www.cambridgeinternational.org/programmes-and-qualifications/cambridge-igcse-mathematics-0580/).
For an examination in **2026**, use the **2025–2027** syllabus rather than
the published future **2028–2030** one. Distinguish **Core/Extended**
entry routes and applicable calculator instructions.

- Reusable math checks: `mc-fraction-units`, `mc-percent-reversal`,
  `mc-similarity-area`.
- Missing: year-appropriate paper-format examples, technology constraints,
  exam-style explanations and Core/Extended curriculum submapping.

## Cambridge AS & A Level Mathematics (9709)

Use the [Cambridge 9709 official syllabus page](https://www.cambridgeinternational.org/programmes-and-qualifications/cambridge-international-as-and-a-level-mathematics-9709/)
and the **2026–2027** version for a 2026 examination. Pure Mathematics,
Mechanics and Probability & Statistics can appear in **different
permitted paper combinations**: never assume all routes include the same
components or that the 2028–2030 syllabus is already in force.

- Existing synthetic check: `mc-endpoint-optimization` for calculus feedback
  and `mc-conditional-normalization` for sample-space reasoning.
- Missing: Mechanics and richer statistics, syllabus-specific pure topics,
  distinct calculator/permitted-technology policies and paper routes.

## A practical reviewer decision

For each synthetic Tutor case:
1. Is its mathematics correct (including domain and quantifiers)?
2. Is this topic in the chosen **exam authority + subject code + year + tier/route**?
3. Is the accepted working compatible with that paper's answer expectations?
4. Did the Tutor adapt to evidence rather than simply reveal the marking answer?
5. Does the assessment claim exceed the present synthetic evidence?

Do **not** use the UK's GCSE year-group labels as global equivalents for US SAT
or Chinese Zhongkao grades, or interchange AQA with Pearson Edexcel/OCR.
