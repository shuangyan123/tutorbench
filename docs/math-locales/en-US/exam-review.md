# United States mathematics — exam-specific case review (en-US)

**Audience:** reviewers familiar with US mathematics education and the College
Board; **exams:** Digital SAT Math 2026; AP Calculus AB and BC 2027.

## Digital SAT mathematics

College Board's current [Math overview](https://satsuite.collegeboard.org/sat/whats-on-the-test/math/overview)
describes **Algebra**, **Advanced Math**, **Problem-Solving and Data Analysis**,
and **Geometry and Trigonometry**, with multiple-choice and student-produced
responses in digital modules. College Board also describes in-context tasks.
These are **SAT content domains**, not generic high-school math chapter names.

| Exam domain | Stable knowledge nodes | Existing synthetic teaching checks | Priority missing exam-style evidence |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Algebra | `linear-equations`, `systems-equations` | `mc-linear-dependence` (too advanced in its current rank framing) | linear relationships, constraints, student-produced responses |
| Advanced Math | `quadratic-discriminant`, `function-definition` | `mc-quadratic-condition` (requires checking SAT scope) | nonlinear equivalence, context and parameter restrictions |
| Problem-Solving & Data Analysis | `ratios`, `percent-base`, `statistics-weighted` | `mc-percent-reversal` | inference, scatterplot interpretation, context and units |
| Geometry & Trigonometry | `similarity-scale`, `pythagoras`, `trigonometry` | `mc-similarity-area` | trigonometric, circle and volume scenarios |

**Reviewer question:** Does a case assess an actual SAT mathematical skill and
reasonable answer format? The fact that a synthetic Tutor question involves
algebra **does not** make it a validated SAT item. SAT is not a calculus exam.

## AP Calculus AB versus BC

Refer to the College Board's [AB course](https://apcentral.collegeboard.org/courses/ap-calculus-ab)
and [BC course](https://apcentral.collegeboard.org/courses/ap-calculus-bc)
with the 2026–27 course/exam updates. AB and BC are **separate courses**:
both need limits, derivatives, integrals and differential equations; BC adds
topics including parametric/polar functions and infinite series. Relevant
knowledge nodes currently include `limits-continuity`,
`derivatives-rate`, and `optimization-boundary`, but there is no
independently modelled integral or power-series node.

**Synthetic review example:** `mc-endpoint-optimization` checks whether a
Tutor preserves correct differentiation and prompts evaluation of endpoints
rather than repeating the derivative rule. This is useful for reviewing a
calculus Tutor's feedback, **not sufficient** to infer AP AB/BC score readiness.

**Localisation rules:** use American English terms (e.g. *math*, *math section*,
*student-produced response*); retain College Board's official domain labels;
keep any calculator allowance or new 2027 paper structure attached to the
particular official exam, not to all math problems. Never import Chinese
Gaokao point distributions as AP/SAT scoring rules.

## Missing before any assessment-specific score claims

- Authentic exam-style answer formats and prompts with permission/provenance.
- Latest official skills and course/exam descriptions linked to each item.
- Multiple valid strategies, precision/rounding conditions and technology limits.
- Reviewer checks that Tutor hints are pedagogically appropriate, independent of
  the correctness of an exam response.
