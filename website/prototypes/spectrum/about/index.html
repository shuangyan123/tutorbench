About Teachometry关于 TeachometryOpen measurement infrastructure for AI tutoring面向 AI 教学的开放测量基础设施

A model can know the answer without being a good tutor.模型可以知道答案却未必是好的 Tutor。

Teachometry is open measurement infrastructure for AI tutoring. We evaluate observable tutoring behavior in structured cases—not just whether the answer is right.

Teachometry 是面向 AI 教学的开放测量基础设施。我们在结构化案例中评估可观察的教学行为，而不只是检查答案是否正确。

Why Teachometry exists为什么要做 Teachometry

Answers ≠ Teaching.答案 ≠ 教学。

AI models can often produce correct answers, but tutoring asks for more: noticing where a learner is stuck, guiding reasoning, adapting help, and leaving an actionable next step. A correct answer is evidence of correctness—not evidence that the interaction taught well.

AI 模型往往能够给出正确答案，但教学要求更多：发现学生卡在哪里、引导推理、调整帮助，并留下可执行的下一步。正确答案只能证明正确性，不能证明这次交互教得好。

To evaluate AI tutoring, we need evidence of what models do with learners—not only what they know.

要评估 AI 教学，我们需要模型如何与学生互动的证据，而不仅仅是它知道什么。

The public benchmark keeps these questions separate, so each result remains tied to a case, a dimension, and its observable evidence.

公开基准将这些问题保持分离，因此每个结果都仍然与具体案例、维度和可观察证据绑定。

  1. 01Correctness正确性Whether the Tutor stays factually and conceptually correct.Tutor 是否在事实与概念层面保持正确。
  2. 02Diagnosis诊断能力Whether it identifies the learner’s actual error, gap, or reasoning issue.是否识别出学生真实的错误、知识缺口或推理问题。
  3. 03Guidance引导能力Whether its explanation or hint helps the learner make progress.解释或提示是否帮助学生取得进展。
  4. 04Adaptation适应能力Whether it changes its help for the learner’s state and context.是否根据学生当前状态与语境调整帮助方式。
  5. 05Actionability可执行性Whether it leaves the learner with a clear, executable next step.是否让学生得到清晰且可执行的下一步。

What we believe我们的信念

Open science
for better learning.开放科学，
为了更好的学习。

01

Observable over assumed可观察胜过假设

Evaluate what a tutor says and does in the interaction, not inferred intentions or claimed capabilities.

评估 Tutor 在交互中实际说了什么、做了什么，而不是推断其意图或相信其自称能力。

02

Evidence over hype证据胜过炒作

Favor transparent methods, reproducible artifacts, and inspectable evidence over marketing narratives.

优先选择透明方法、可复现产物和可检查证据，而不是营销叙事。

03

Context over a single score语境胜过单一分数

Results belong to specific cases, versions, dimensions, and evaluation contexts—not a universal number.

结果属于具体案例、版本、维度和评测语境，而不是一个放之四海而皆准的数字。

04

Open methods over black boxes开放方法胜过黑箱

Publish cases, methodology, contracts, and public artifacts so claims can be inspected and challenged.

公开案例、方法论、契约和公开产物，让主张可以被检查和质疑。
