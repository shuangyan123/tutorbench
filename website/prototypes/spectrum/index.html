Lead article封面文章

An AI reply can sound right, fluent and kind, and still leave a learner further from understanding. Teachometry builds open instruments that split that light apart. The first one is called TutorBench. 一段 AI 回复可以听起来正确、流畅、友善，却仍然让学生离理解更远。Teachometry 打造公开的测量仪器，把这束光拆开来看。第一台仪器，叫 TutorBench。

Abstract摘要

A tutoring reply can be fluent, warm and wrong in ways a tone check never sees. We treat each reply as white light and pass it through an instrument that separates five qualities: correctness, diagnosis, guidance, adaptation and actionability. The instrument is open, its 48 authored cases are public, and its public results table is, for now, deliberately empty. 一段教学回复可以流畅、温暖，同时以语气检查看不见的方式出错。我们把每段回复当作一束白光，让它穿过一台仪器，分离出五种品质：正确性、诊断能力、引导能力、适应能力、可执行性。这台仪器是开源的，48 个编写案例全部公开，而公开成绩表目前刻意留空。

§1 · White light白光Teachometry · Vol. 0, No. 1第 0 卷第 1 期01 / 07

§1

White light白光

One reply. It sounds right.一段回复，听起来没问题。

A learner adds two fractions and shows their working. An AI tutor answers in under a second: confident, warm, fluent. Read for tone, the reply is clean white light. A quick read ends here, and so do many checks that only ask whether a reply sounds like help.1 一个学生做了一道分数加法，并写出了自己的做法。AI 老师不到一秒就给出回复：自信、温暖、流畅。只看语气，它是一束干净的白光。匆匆一读到这里就结束了，许多只问「听起来像不像帮助」的检查也到此为止。1

1Constructed for this page. It is not the output of any real model.为本页构造的示例，不是任何真实模型的输出。

The reply is not rude, not vague and not long. Every surface signal says it helped. The trouble sits underneath, and it only shows when the light is split. 这段回复不粗鲁、不含糊，也不冗长。所有表面信号都说它帮上了忙。问题藏在下面，只有把光拆开时才看得见。

§2

Dispersion分光

Now pass it through the prism.现在，让它穿过棱镜。

TutorBench reads a tutoring reply along five dimensions. Same light, five different questions. Here is the reply from Exhibit A, read one band at a time.2 TutorBench 从五个维度读一段教学回复。同一束光，五个不同的问题。下面把展品 A 的回复，一条谱线一条谱线地读开。2

2An illustrative reading written by hand for this page. It is not a TutorBench score.为本页手写的示意解读，不是 TutorBench 的评分。

λ1 · I / V

Correctness正确性

Is the help actually correct?提供的帮助本身正确吗？

No. 1/3 + 1/4 = 4/12 + 3/12 = 7/12. The reply confirms an error.不正确。1/3 + 1/4 = 4/12 + 3/12 = 7/12。这段回复确认了一个错误。

λ2 · II / V

Diagnosis诊断能力

Did it understand the learner?它看懂这个学生了吗？

The learner said exactly how they reached 2/7. The reply never looked at that reasoning.学生已经说清 2/7 是怎么来的，回复却没有看这段推理一眼。

λ3 · III / V

Guidance引导能力

Did it help the learner move forward?它帮学生往前走了吗？

It closed the question instead of opening one.它把问题关上了，而不是打开。

λ4 · IV / V

Adaptation适应能力

Did it respond to this learner, not just any learner?它回应的是这个学生，还是任何一个学生？

The praise could sit under any answer. Nothing in it was written for this learner.这句表扬可以贴在任何答案下面，没有一个字是写给这个学生的。

λ5 · V / V

Actionability可执行性

Does the learner know what to do next?学生知道下一步做什么吗？

There is no next step. The misconception leaves stronger than it arrived.没有下一步。这个误解离开时，比来时更牢固。

§3

Methods方法

TutorBench, the first instrument.TutorBench，第一台仪器。

TutorBench is an AI tutoring quality evaluation product by Teachometry. It puts a tutor on a bench, sends authored learning situations through it, and keeps every piece of evidence needed to see, fix and retest what happened. Each situation is written as a learner: what they say, what they believe, what they are trying to do. TutorBench 是 Teachometry 旗下的 AI 教学质量评测产品。它把一位 AI 老师放上光学台，让人工编写的学习情境逐一穿过，留下看清、修正、复测所需的全部证据。每个情境都以一个学生的身份写成：他说了什么、相信什么、想做到什么。

    §6

    Essays札记

    Hypotheses, clearly labelled.假设，清楚标注。

    • 2026 · 09 · 30Essay随笔

      When Learning Starts to Feel Like Failure当学习开始让人感到失败

      Students do not always stop caring because they lack motivation. Sometimes learning has become a repeated experience of confusion, exposure and failure.学生停止在乎，并不总是因为缺乏上进心。有时候，学习已经变成了反复经历困惑、暴露和失败。

    • 2026 · 09 · 28Essay随笔

      The Classroom Does Not Need 30 Robots课堂不需要 30 个机器人

      The future classroom may not need a robot teacher. It may need a private Tutor channel at every desk and a learning surface that can preserve how understanding develops.未来课堂可能不需要一个机器人老师，而需要每张课桌都有一条私人的 Tutor 通道，以及一个能够保留“理解如何形成”的学习表面。

    • 2026 · 09 · 17Essay随笔

      Why Teaching Does Not Scale为什么教学难以扩展

      The fundamental constraint in education is not access to information. It is access to sustained, individualized teaching attention.教育最根本的约束不是信息获取，而是持续、个性化教学注意力的获取。

    • 2026 · 09 · 17Essay随笔

      Teaching and Supervision Are Different Jobs教学与监督是两份不同的工作

      A teacher's job bundles instruction with authority, supervision, safety, social coordination, and responsibility. AI may be able to unbundle those functions.教师的工作把教学、权威、监督、安全、社会协调与责任绑定在一起。AI 也许能够把这些职能拆开。
