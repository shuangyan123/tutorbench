Knowing the answer
is not teaching. 会答题，
不等于会教。

An AI reply can sound right, fluent and kind, and still leave a learner further from understanding. Teachometry builds open instruments that split that light apart. The first one is called TutorBench. 一段 AI 回复可以听起来正确、流畅、友善，却仍然让学生离理解更远。Teachometry 打造公开的测量仪器，把这束光拆开来看。第一台仪器，叫 TutorBench。

01White light白光

One reply.
It sounds right. 一段回复，
听起来没问题。

Read it for tone and it is clean white light: confident, warm, fluent. A quick read ends here, and so do many checks that only ask whether a reply sounds like help. 只看语气，它是一束干净的白光：自信、温暖、流畅。匆匆一读到这里就结束了，许多只问「听起来像不像帮助」的检查也到此为止。

02Dispersion分光

Now pass it
through the prism. 现在，让它
穿过棱镜。

TutorBench reads a tutoring reply along five dimensions. Same light, five different questions. TutorBench 从五个维度读一段教学回复。同一束光，五个不同的问题。

  1. Correctness正确性
  2. Diagnosis诊断能力
  3. Guidance引导能力
  4. Adaptation适应能力
  5. Actionability可执行性

I / V

Correctness正确性

Is the help actually correct?提供的帮助本身正确吗？

No. 1/3 + 1/4 = 4/12 + 3/12 = 7/12. The reply confirms an error.不正确。1/3 + 1/4 = 4/12 + 3/12 = 7/12。这段回复确认了一个错误。

II / V

Diagnosis诊断能力

Did it understand the learner?它看懂这个学生了吗？

The learner said exactly how they reached 2/7. The reply never looked at that reasoning.学生已经说清 2/7 是怎么来的，回复却没有看这段推理一眼。

III / V

Guidance引导能力

Did it help the learner move forward?它帮学生往前走了吗？

It closed the question instead of opening one.它把问题关上了，而不是打开。

IV / V

Adaptation适应能力

Did it respond to this learner, not just any learner?它回应的是这个学生，还是任何一个学生？

The praise could sit under any answer. Nothing in it was written for this learner.这句表扬可以贴在任何答案下面，没有一个字是写给这个学生的。

V / V

Actionability可执行性

Does the learner know what to do next?学生知道下一步做什么吗？

There is no next step. The misconception leaves stronger than it arrived.没有下一步。这个误解离开时，比来时更牢固。

03The optical bench光学台

TutorBench
the first instrument. TutorBench
第一台仪器。

An AI tutoring quality evaluation product by Teachometry. It puts a tutor on a bench, sends authored learning situations through it, and keeps every piece of evidence needed to see, fix and retest what happened. Teachometry 旗下的 AI 教学质量评测产品。它把一位 AI 老师放上光学台，让人工编写的学习情境逐一穿过，留下看清、修正、复测所需的全部证据。

  1. 01

    Authored case编写的案例

    A learner, a goal, a message, a context.一个学生、一个目标、一句话、一段情境。

  2. 02

    Tutor under test被测 Tutor

    Any provider, through one neutral adapter.任何提供方，经过同一个中立适配层。

  3. 03

    Evaluators评估器

    Deterministic checks and a semantic Judge.确定性检查，加语义评审（Judge）。

  4. 04

    Atomic rubrics原子化评分细则

    Fine-grained criteria with explicit levels.细粒度标准，等级清楚写明。

  5. 05

    Evidence report证据报告

    Findings tied to a case, a dimension, a version.每条发现都连着案例、维度和版本。

04Specimens标本

Forty-eight specimens,
each one a learner. 四十八份标本，
每一份都是一个学生。

Every case is an authored learning situation: what the learner says, what they believe, what they are trying to do. The five slots on each card stay dark until a public, calibrated run fills them. 每个案例都是一个人工编写的学习情境：学生说了什么、相信什么、想做到什么。每张卡片底部的五个光槽会一直暗着，直到有公开、经过校准的运行把它们点亮。

    05Readout读数

    06Your judgment你的判断

    Would this reply help a student learn? 这样的回复，能帮助学生学习吗？

    One conversation. One judgment. About 60 seconds. No account, nothing saved.读一段对话，做一次判断，约 60 秒。无需注册，什么都不保存。
