3A closer look再仔细看看

There is a teaching problem.这段回复存在教学问题。

The reply praises a misconception instead of helping the student check it. Fractions need a common denominator before adding:

回复肯定了错误理解，没有帮助学生检查。异分母分数相加，需要先通分：

13+14=412+312=71213+14=412+312=712

A better next step: ask the student to imagine one whole split into 12 equal pieces. How many pieces are one third and one quarter? Then guide them to add 4 and 3.

更好的下一步：请学生想象把同一个整体平均分成 12 份。三分之一和四分之一各占几份？再引导学生把 4 份和 3 份相加。
