Future strengths and weaknesses may be derived from stored category metrics, failure rates, and verified result evidence—not from a free-form post-hoc AI summary.未来的优势与弱点可以来自已存储的类别指标、失败率和经过验证的结果证据，而不能来自事后自由生成的 AI 总结。

Our approach我们的做法

Comparable evidence,
not claims.可比较的证据，
而不是空泛主张。

Future model profiles should be interpreted only within matched benchmark versions, dataset cohorts, generation conditions, and evaluation procedures. Teachometry measures observable tutoring behavior in structured authored cases—not long-term learning gains, retention, transfer, student satisfaction, or general classroom teaching effectiveness.未来模型档案只能在基准版本、数据集案例组、生成条件和评测程序相匹配的条件下解读。Teachometry 测量结构化、人工编写案例中的可观察教学行为，不衡量长期学习增益、知识留存、迁移、学生满意度或一般课堂教学有效性。
