Teachometry builds tools to understand and improve AI teaching quality. We also collaborate with teams on evaluation, technical research, advisory work, and selected engineering projects.

Teachometry 打造用于理解和改进 AI 教学质量的工具。我们也与团队合作，开展评测、技术研究、顾问工作，以及有选择的工程项目。

What we do我们做什么

Evaluation first.以评测为核心。

AI education evaluation is the core of our work. Research and advisory grow out of it, and engineering projects are taken on selectively.

AI 教育评测是我们工作的核心。研究与顾问由它延伸而来，工程项目则有选择地承接。

Core核心

AI education evaluationAI 教育评测

For teams building AI tutors and AI education products, education technology companies, and research groups.

面向 AI Tutor 与 AI 教育产品的开发团队、教育科技企业和研究机构。

  1. 01

    Teaching-quality evaluation and independent reviewAI 教学质量评测与独立审查

    How your AI tutor behaves with learners, observed across structured cases. We can also act as an independent reviewer of its teaching.

    在结构化案例中观察你的 AI Tutor 面对学习者时的实际表现。我们也可以作为独立方，审查它的教学。

  2. 02

    Custom scenarios and evaluation criteria定制评测场景与评价标准

    Scenarios built around your subject, your learners and your product, with the criteria agreed before any results are seen.

    围绕你的学科、学习者和产品设计场景；评价标准在看到任何结果之前就先商定。

  3. 03

    Diagnosis, evidence reports and regression tests教学行为诊断、证据报告与回归测试

    Where the teaching goes wrong, shown case by case with the evidence, then kept as tests you can run again after every change.

    逐个案例指出教学在哪里出了问题、附上证据，再把它们留作每次改动后都能重跑的测试。

  4. 04

    Product-quality research and evaluation partnershipsAI 教育产品质量研究与评测合作

    Longer studies of the quality of AI education products, carried out together with your team.

    与你的团队一起，开展更长期的 AI 教育产品质量研究。

Collaboration专业合作

Research & advisory研究与顾问

AI system evaluation, technical research, product-quality advice, prototype validation, research collaboration, and ongoing advisory work.

AI 系统评价、技术研究、产品质量咨询、原型验证、研究合作，以及持续的顾问服务。

Supplementary补充

Selected engineering work有选择的工程项目

We take on a small number of remote software projects: API integrations, AI tools, automation and technical delivery. This work helps fund the research, so we only accept what leaves room for it.

我们也少量承接远程软件工程项目：API 集成、AI 工具、自动化和技术交付。这些工作为研究提供资金支持，所以我们只接不挤占研究的项目。

Not sure where your question fits? Bring it as it is. Working out the scope together is part of the work.

不确定你的问题属于哪一类？照原样带来就好。一起把范围理清，本身就是合作的一部分。

How we work合作流程

From a first conversation to delivery.从第一次交流，到最终交付。

Every engagement is scoped with you and agreed in writing before work starts.

每一次合作都先和你一起确定范围，并在开工前写成书面协议。

  1. 01

    Discuss沟通需求Discuss

    You tell us what you are building and what you need to know. We ask questions until the problem is clear.

    你告诉我们你在做什么、想弄清楚什么。我们会一直追问，直到问题清楚为止。

  2. 02

    Scope确定范围Scope

    Together we settle the scope, the deliverables, the timeline and the fee.

    我们一起确定范围、交付物、时间和费用。

  3. 03

    Agreement签订协议Agreement

    The terms are put in writing and agreed by both sides before any work begins.

    条款写成书面协议，双方确认后才开始工作。

  4. 04

    Delivery交付成果Delivery

    We carry out the work as agreed and hand over what was promised, with the evidence behind it.

    按协议开展工作，交付约定的成果，并附上背后的证据。

Forms of engagement合作形式

By project按项目
One defined piece of work with a fixed scope.一项范围明确的工作。
In milestones分阶段里程碑
Larger work in stages, each one agreed and delivered in turn.较大的工作分成几个阶段，逐段商定、逐段交付。
Ongoing advisory长期顾问
Regular advice over a longer period.在较长时间内定期提供建议。

Which of these we can offer depends on the work and on our capacity at the time; not every form is always available.

能提供哪种形式，取决于具体工作和我们当时的安排，并非每种形式都随时可选。

We don’t publish fixed prices, and nothing is sold online. Fees are agreed for each piece of work.

我们不公开固定价格，也不提供在线购买。费用针对每一项具体工作商定。

Independence and boundaries独立性与边界

Fees pay for the work,
not for the findings.费用买的是工作，
不是结论。

These rules apply to every engagement, whoever it is with.

无论与谁合作，这些规则都适用。

  1. § 1

    Findings are not for sale.结论不出售。

    Independent research and public results are never tied to sponsorship or consulting fees. An evaluation reports what the evidence shows, whoever pays for it.

    独立研究和公开结果从不与赞助或咨询报酬挂钩。无论谁付费，评测都只报告证据显示的内容。

  2. § 2

    Two roles, kept apart.两种角色，分开说明。

    Evaluating a product independently and helping to build it are different roles, and they can conflict. We say up front which role we are in, and if we have helped build something, we say so before we evaluate it.

    独立评测一个产品，和参与开发它，是两种不同的角色，可能彼此冲突。我们会事先说明自己处于哪种角色；如果参与过开发，会在评测之前说明。

  3. § 3

    TutorBench stays whole.TutorBench 保持完整。

    An engagement does not transfer the intellectual property of TutorBench as a whole. What each project delivers, and who owns it, is set out in the agreement.

    一次合作不会转让 TutorBench 整体的知识产权。每个项目交付什么、归谁所有，都写在协议里。

  4. § 4

    Honest about the instrument.如实说明工具的局限。

    TutorBench is a Developer Preview. Its results are not yet calibrated, and it cannot show that learners learn more. No report of ours will claim otherwise.

    TutorBench 仍是开发者预览版。它的结果尚未校准，也不能证明学习者因此学得更好。我们的任何报告都不会作出相反的声明。
